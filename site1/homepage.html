Life as a CS student in TCGC

Having only spent a few months here, it's not so bad. This place is quite far from my hometown so studying here
gave me an opportunity to meet new people. Some if not most students here came from different provinces/cities
so it's not unusual that new friend groups form naturally. Classes in college are definitely different from high school.
Back then, when a subject teacher is absent and doesn't have a substitute, you're basically stuck until the next period.
Now, you can just walk out and come back when the next class starts. It feels refreshing. As someone who came from
the ICT strand back in high school, I sorta have an advantage in our major courses. I'm fairly familiar with computers
and have a decent background with front-end work. We have two majors by the way Prog 1 and IntroCom. Programming 1
can feel abit intimidating as it's a new environment for me but it's not as far off from my past javascript experience.
Introduction to Computing in the other hand feels like a breeze. It's basically just a recap of what I already learned a
year ago. I don't know about our next term though, we're now gonna be tackling binary ( math seriously hurts my brain ).
My overall experience here isn't that bad. Aside from the 30 minute commute. It really sucks when class ends at 7PM, the
bus could either arrive early or by the next hour so yeah.. that's awfully late. I seriously hope my choices wouldn't
disappoint me in the near future though. I don't know why I entered CS knowing full well I suck at math but here we are.
No turning back now.

What I expect in this program

TONS OF MATH
I expect this course to he alot more heavy on maths since it delves deep into the nitty gritty of computers ( base 2 digits ), and calculus too for
advance programming algorithms. As someone that's bad at math, this will definitely be challening.
LOTS OF CODING
What else should we expect? obviously CODING. There's one course dedicated to it so it's painfully obvious that this will be abundant throughout
the whole 4 years of hell. Coding isn't that bad however, trying to think of a logical solution to code is what makes it frustrating.
TECH HEAVY
The name of the program has the word "computer", it should be self explanatory that having tech literacy is a must. It's expected that we're gonna
be dealing with networks, systems, and other related concepts.
